# High-stress SAC305 constitutive literature audit

**Phase 0L, Part VII.** Is there primary SAC305 constitutive data covering the stress regime
the package actually occupies — roughly 15–100+ MPa at 45–150 °C?

Artifacts: `results/phase0l/high_stress_sac305_sources.csv`,
`results/phase0l/lit/raw_hits.json`, `scripts/phase0l/lit_search.py`,
`scripts/phase0l/lit_audit_table.py`, retrieved full texts under `results/phase0l/lit/`.

## 1. How the search was performed, and its limits

The `WebSearch` tool is disabled in this environment by organisation policy: a call returns
`constraints/vertexai.allowedPartnerModelFeatures violated … disallowed feature web_search`.
Ordinary HTTPS egress does work. The search was therefore run directly against the open
scholarly APIs, which is reproducible and citable:

| API | Endpoint | Status |
|---|---|---|
| OpenAlex | `api.openalex.org/works` | reachable |
| Crossref | `api.crossref.org/works` | reachable |
| Europe PMC | `ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest` | reachable |
| Semantic Scholar | `api.semanticscholar.org` | HTTP 429, not used |

Eight queries were run against three APIs
(`scripts/phase0l/lit_search.py`, queries listed in the file):
**344 unique works**, of which **115** are flagged open access. Title screening for
alloy + constitutive content gives **89 candidates**.

**Retrieval, not discovery, is the binding constraint.** The relevant corpus is dominated by
IEEE (ITherm, ECTC, EuroSimE) and ASME (IPACK, IMECE) conference papers. Direct fetches were
attempted and refused: MDPI returns HTTP 403 to both the article and PDF endpoints,
`matec-conferences.org` returns 403, and the IEEE `ielx` PDF endpoint returns 502. Where MDPI
content is mirrored in PMC it was retrievable, and Europe PMC full-text XML worked well.

**Five sources were retrieved and read in full.** The other 84 are recorded in the CSV with
tier `SCREENED` and every content field set to `not retrieved`. They are not evidence.

## 2. What the search found

The corpus is large and directly on point — this is not a case of the data not existing. Highly
relevant titles include, among many:

- *The effects of aging on the Anand viscoplastic constitutive model for SAC305 solder*
  (ITherm 2014, doi:10.1109/itherm.2014.6892272)
- *High strain rate constitutive behavior of SAC105 and SAC305 leadfree solder during operation*
  (Microelectron. Reliab. 2016, doi:10.1016/j.microrel.2016.03.014)
- *Determination of Anand Parameters From Creep Testing of SAC305 Solder Joints*
  (IPACK 2020, doi:10.1115/ipack2020-2690)
- *Isothermal Aging Dependent Anand Parameters of SAC305 Lead Free Solder at Extreme High
  Temperature* (ITherm 2020, doi:10.1109/itherm45881.2020.9190596)
- *Thermoviscoplastic Response and Constitutive Modeling of SAC305 Lead-Free Solder over Wide
  Ranges…* (2026, doi:10.2139/ssrn.7320629)
- *Constitutive and Aging Behavior of Sn3.0Ag0.5Cu Solder Alloy*
  (IEEE TEPM 2009, doi:10.1109/tepm.2009.2024119)

**None of these could be retrieved here.** Their existence is recorded so that a reader with
institutional access can close this gap quickly; it is the single highest-value unblocking
action available to this project.

| Source | Alloy | T (°C) | σ (MPa) | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Yuan 2023, Materials 16(14):4922 | mixed — see below | 45–150 / −40–122 | 7.51–15.92 | the source of the current card |
| Xiao & Armstrong 2005 (via Yuan Tables 5, 6) | **Sn3.9Ag0.6Cu** | 45–150 | 7.51–15.92 | **wrong alloy**, low stress |
| Motalab et al. 2012 (via Yuan Tables 7, 9) | SAC305 | −40–122 | tensile levels | correct alloy, incomplete parameters |
| Micromachines 9(11):608 (2018) | SAC305 | 27 only | ~25 | room temperature only |
| Materials 14(20):5973 (2021) | SAC | room temp | **< 12 by construction** | authors restrict it below 12 MPa |

### The alloy split inside the current card

Yuan 2023 is the single source behind both halves of the SAC305 card, and the two halves do not
come from the same alloy:

- **Creep** (the 19 points and the Garofalo constants) is Xiao & Armstrong's
  **Sn3.9Ag0.6Cu** — 3.9 wt % Ag against SAC305's 3.0 wt %.
- **Plasticity** (Chaboche σ₀(T)) traces to Motalab's **SAC305** measurements, through an Anand
  fit and then synthetic stress–strain curves generated by that Anand model, and is therefore
  two model layers from any measurement.

The brief forbids transplanting high-Ag SAC parameter sets without explicit transferability
evidence. **No transferability evidence was found.** No retrieved source compares SAC305 and
Sn3.9Ag0.6Cu creep under common conditions.

## 4. Answer to the Part VII question

**No.** No primary SAC305 constitutive dataset covering 15–100+ MPa at 45–150 °C was retrievable
in this environment.

The closest available object is the **nine-parameter Anand set for SAC305** (Motalab et al., as
tabulated in Yuan 2023 Tables 7 and 9). It is the correct alloy and it does reach tensile stress
levels, but three things block it:

1. Yuan does not report Motalab's activation energy, so that column is incomplete. Only Yuan's
   own EINN / EINN+BI columns are fully specified (Q = 65 kJ/mol, stated in §4.2).
2. Its source data could not be retrieved, so the Part X reproduction test cannot be run
   against the data it was actually fitted to.
3. It was calibrated at strain rates of 10⁻³–10⁻⁵ s⁻¹. Extrapolated down to the 10⁻⁹–10⁻⁷ s⁻¹
   regime of the only creep data available, it is **1240× too fast** at the median
   (Part X, TEST 1). The two datasets that exist for this alloy family bracket the operating
   regime from opposite sides and overlap nowhere.

## 5. Consequence

The high-stress constitutive response of SAC305 cannot be closed from the evidence available
here. It can only be **bounded**, and Part X shows the bound is very wide: candidate laws that
all fit the low-stress data to within a factor of 2.6 diverge by **six to sixteen orders of
magnitude** at 20–120 MPa.
