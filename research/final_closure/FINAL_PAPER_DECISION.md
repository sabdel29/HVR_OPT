## Scenario evidentiary weight

## Defects found in this closure

- **DEFECT #65** (local solder post-processing): the validated .dat parser keyed output by its 7-significant-digit printed time and averaged blocks sharing a key; sub-millisecond increments at a load step biased the LAST cycle (up to -17 %). Found by the acceptance test of policy amendment 1; fixed by block-order extraction (scripts/final_closure_local_extract.py), which reproduces the published control to all ten printed digits and leaves every earlier production value unchanged (local_mesh_policy.json amendment 3).
- **DEFECT #66** (Z2 out-of-cell construction), RESOLVED IN THE BASELINE: the earlier encapsulated model filled the PCB opening up to the board sidewall, bonding the backside converter to the board although the stack specifies a 1.0 mm clearance. The production geometry now voids that ring (F1_clearance); the sidewall-filled construction survives only as the class-B variant f1_sidewall_filled.
- **DEFECT #67** (BGA-fed control only): in Z2_BGA_FED_CONTROL the 48 V enters at the land-side RDL but the intra-VR model places VIN on the high-side RDL, so a ~1.3 mm HV vertical traverse is not modelled. Z1 and the direct-fed Z2 take 48 V at the high-side RDL, where the model places it.
- **DEFECT #68** (E-T coupling), FIXED IN EVERY CASE: the converter devices deposit their loss only inside the 49 cell footprints, but the loss model was fed the node mean of the WHOLE converter tiers, encapsulant included -- 7.8 K below the in-cell mean for the direct-fed Z2 and 1.2 K above it for Z1 at HV-S0 (found on the first corrected-lineage results). Every ledger temperature is now the mean over the region its terms deposit in, each device term at its own tier's in-cell temperature (final_closure_physical_etm.temperature_regions), and every case was re-run. The pre-fix HV-S0 cases and the diagnostic are in research/final_closure/superseded_pre_tile_temperature/.
- **DEFECT #69** (solver harness), FIXED: the production local-solder runner accepted whatever a .dat held. When the per-user thread limit of the shared host was reached, CalculiX died mid-run; a control solve cut off at 1800.5 s of 2400 s returned d_eps = 2.695e-3 against its published 6.513e-3 (the control check stopped that run), and one production solve returned no response. A local solve is now accepted only if its last block sits at the schedule end, stale outputs are removed before every solve, and a solid-solder joint without a response stops the run. Every class-B and local solve that overlapped the exhaustion window was discarded and re-run. The two r31 ladder cases had one intermediate E-T iterate (it2) in it; their reported state comes from later solves, and the iterate changes contract geometrically through it (4.1, 0.57, 0.079, 0.011 K for Z2; 2.8, 0.32, 0.037, 0.0043 K for Z1).
- **Superseded primary Z2**: the earlier primary Z2 was BGA-fed (48 V through peripheral package balls and a package 48 V RDL), had no explicit VR attach and was sidewall-filled. Its case files not re-run here are kept in research/final_closure/superseded_bga_fed_z2/ for provenance only.
