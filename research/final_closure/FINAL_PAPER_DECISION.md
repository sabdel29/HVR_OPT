## What remains unqualified (does not invalidate the paper)

- the 48 V input waveform: results are conditioned on named scenarios HV-S0/S1/S2;
- the 1.5 A/ball criterion: an engineering provisioning rule, not an allowable;
- absolute solder life: response only, per Darveaux (10.1115/1.1413764);
- EM-safe current densities for balls, pads, TSVs: none claimed;
- joint temperatures at or above the SAC305 melting onset (about 217 C) mark a physically inadmissible operating point, not a response: Z1 HV_S2 (243 C);
- joint temperatures above the Norton card's 125 C calibration are flagged as extrapolation (Z1 HV_S1, Z1 HV_S2, Z2 HV_S2).

Active manuscript carries the physical lineage (macros, physical-comparison section, abstract): **True**.
