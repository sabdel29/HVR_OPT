# Phase 0N-AJ findings

Generated by `scripts/phase0naj_write_findings.py` from the artifacts listed at

## 1. What changed relative to `20dff841`

Three defects were found, two of them invalidating results reported in that
commit.

**A floating-point bug hid 79 % of the BGA design space.** `bga_lattice_sites()`
built coordinates as `-14.4 + i * 0.8`, which accumulates error, so the lattice
was not closed under negation: the mirror of `(-14.4, -12.8)` existed as
`(14.4, 12.800000000000002)`. `select_power_sites()` tests `mirror not in cand`
with exact tuple equality, so it silently discarded every site whose partner had
drifted --- 1080 of 1369 on the full lattice and 408 of 528 on the Z2 annulus.
Every BGA selection, every package-RDL resistance and every controlled cutout
experiment in `20dff841` was computed on an arbitrary ~21 % subset chosen by
floating-point luck. Coordinates are now snapped; closure is asserted by test.

**The BGA count was sized by a criterion its own network disproves.** 16+16 came
from `I_total / N_ball <= 1.5 A/ball`, which presumes equal sharing. The
voltage-fed network shows sharing is strongly unequal, and the peak ball current
is 9.52x
the equal-share value at worst.

**R1 obstacle routing could detect a blockage and charge no detour.** Blockage
used component extents; the detour magnitude searched for component centres.
Two further errors sat underneath: a component containing a terminal counted as
blocking the route starting inside it, and the `vout_plane` self-net was fed to
a point-to-point router at all.

## 6. Pad count: an admissible region, not an optimum

Floor 4, geometric ceiling
4908, loss span
1227x.

| constraint | J limit A/cm^2 | admissible N |
|---|---|---|
| `J_NONE` | None | [4, 4908] |
| `J_1e4` | 10000.0 | [4096, 4908] |
| `J_1e5` | 100000.0 | [256, 4908] |
| `J_1e6` | 1000000.0 | [32, 4908] |

NON-PHYSICAL. At the voltage-drop floor N = 4 each pad carries 5.143 A through 100 um^2, i.e. 5.14e+06 A/cm^2 -- about 5x the ~1e6 A/cm^2 at which Cu-Cu bump electromigration is studied under ACCELERATED stress. The drop budget is therefore not the binding constraint anywhere in this design space: current density is. The old 'voltage-drop floor of 4 pads/rail/cell' was never a candidate design point, only an artefact of applying one criterion in isolation.

## 7. Intra-VR fidelity ladder

R2 is now compared against R0 for the first time. R0 overstates the output-net
loss by between 37.2 % and
258.3 %, so **R0 is not adequate for that
net**. R2 is the HIGHER-FIDELITY MODEL HERE, not the truth. It solves the same 2-D sheet physics with real terminal footprints; it is not an independent solver and not an experiment, so agreement with it is CLOSED_FORM/MODEL_CONSISTENCY, never validation
