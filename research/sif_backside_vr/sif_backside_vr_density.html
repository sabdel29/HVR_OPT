Si-IF chiplet system · backside power delivery · 25 November 2026

How much current can off-the-shelf converters deliver from the back of a Si-IF?

Converters are mounted side by side on the wafer backside and the current reaches the chiplets through TWVs: a 1 V rail, 1 kA, from 48 V or 12 V. Current density is load current per mm² of backside area taken by the converters.

Short answer

  • Yes, 1 kA per 1000 mm² works from both 48 V and 12 V with catalog parts on one layer. A GaN hybrid switched-capacitor buck built from catalog parts reaches it at 86% (48 V) and 87% (12 V) conversion efficiency, about 3 points lower when hot.
  • The practical ceiling is about 1.3–1.4 A/mm² at 84–86%. Past that, only the smallest chip inductors still fit, running at their current limits, and efficiency drops off a cliff.
  • The absolute maximum is 1.6–1.7 A/mm² with gate drivers and local decoupling on the same layer, or 2.1–2.2 A/mm² for the power stage alone. Both run near 70% efficiency and 57–96 W/cm² of heat, so they are bounds, not designs.
  • At 12 V, catalog modules match the custom design: integrated-inductor modules (MPS, Infineon) give 1.0 A/mm² at 87%. At 48 V, no catalog route gets close: a 48→12 V bus converter plus those modules reaches 0.56 A/mm² at 86%, and the custom single-stage hybrid nearly doubles that.
  • Cost per kA: about $150 in parts for the 1 A/mm² discrete designs (controller not included), $220–270 for 12 V modules at 1 A/mm², $380–440 for the 48 V catalog two-stage route at equal efficiency, and about $1,050 for Vicor’s catalog PRM + VTM.
  • TWVs are not the limit. About 2,700 copper vias of 50 µm carry 1 kA with 2–12 W of loss. Spreading the current in the backside copper costs more, 10–60 W.

Getting 1 kA through the wafer

Through-wafer vias

About 2,700 copper TWVs carry 1 kA: 50 µm in diameter, 0.75 A each, for the 1 V rail and its return. At a 125 µm pitch they occupy about 40 mm², 4% of a 1000 mm² footprint. Through 100, 300 and 700 µm of silicon they lose 1.7, 5.0 and 11.8 W.

Backside copper

Spreading each converter’s output to the vias under it costs 10–60 W per kA at 1 A/mm², set by the copper thickness (20–50 µm) and the number of output feeds per cell. Smaller cells lower it. End to end, the 1 A/mm² designs deliver 81–85% at 48 V and 83–86% at 12 V, before the top-side distribution.

What the numbers include

Area

Converter parts on one layer with 100 µm between part bodies, plus gate drivers, bootstrap and input capacitors. Modules count as 1.3 × their footprint for tiling gaps and input capacitors. With 200 µm spacing the maximum density falls by about 12%.

Efficiency

Loss model at 25 °C; hot resistances cost about 3 points. Dead time, inductor AC winding loss, solder and board resistance and controller power are not modeled. At 12 V the measured module curve and the modeled discrete design agree within half a point at 1 A/mm².

Heat

At 1 A/mm² the converters dissipate 150–160 W per kA, about 15–16 W/cm² of backside area. At the maximum density it is 57–63 W/cm². Thermal limits are not modeled.

Part limits

The 1 A/mm² designs run the 17 nH chip inductor near its ratings: 54 A peak against 60 A saturation, 37 A RMS against 45 A. Rating the inductors at 20% inductance drop instead of 30% lowers the power-stage maximum to 1.80 A/mm² (48 V) and 1.67 A/mm² (12 V); efficiency at 1 A/mm² moves by less than half a point. Halving or doubling the core loss moves it by up to about 1 point.

Height

The 1 A/mm² designs use parts at most 3 mm tall. The 96–97% designs need 11–12 mm tall inductors. The catalog modules are 5–10 mm tall.

Control

The hybrid topologies need a controller that drives the flying-capacitor switches in sequence. Standard multiphase buck controllers do not, so plan a programmable or custom controller.

Prices

EPC list price at 2,500 units, other discrete parts near 1,000 units, modules at 800–8,000 units from distributors. Volume contracts are lower. Vicor MCM/GCM, Ferric and Empower high-current parts have no public prices.

Method

HVR_OPT synthesis limited to one component tier: buck and N:1 switched-capacitor hybrids (N up to 8 at 48 V, 5 at 12 V), 4–250 cells per kA, 0.1–3 MHz. Libraries: EPC catalog GaN transistors with the framework’s voltage-stress and derating rules, 24 Murata MLCCs with DC-bias data, 120 catalog inductors (17 nH–1 µH) held to their datasheet saturation and RMS ratings. Every placement is checked for overlap.
